How the export was read

  • Dr. Dang's appointments, Sept 2025 to Sept 2026: 7,790 rows, plus 2,388 for Dr. Iizuka. Monday and Wednesday clinics (93 days) drive the template. Thursdays and surgery days are counted separately.
  • Consults with cataract, RLE, IOLX or refractive terms in the reason are counted as r. Other consults are c. That split matches the OD: 60% of r went to TG, 84% of c to GT or DP.
  • New consults are pooled across both doctors, since either can take a new referral. Follow-ups, post-ops and urgents are counted for Dr. Dang only, because each doctor keeps her own follow-up patients.
  • Follow-ups with "CAT R/A" are reassessments. Follow-ups marked "TRIAGE" are counted as urgents.
  • The export has no appointment status, so no-shows and cancellations can't be separated from patients seen.
